From the owner

Twenty years behind the same counter

I opened this shop in Mohalla Riaz Abad twenty years ago, and my day still starts in the dark before fajr. I remember the first winter, when a few neighbours came by at dawn just for warm bread and stayed to talk. Many of those families still come in every morning.

I named the shop after my son Hammad. I want him to grow up seeing that a shop is built on people trusting you, not on a signboard. That is why the milk is fresh, the yoghurt is set daily and the shelves stay stocked.

We are a small shop, so we know most of our customers by name. If something is not right, tell me directly and I will fix it.
